# Sunday, March 15

Woke up late today. The rain against the window made it impossible to
get out of bed before ten. Made coffee the way I like it — pour-over,
medium grind, exactly 200 degrees. Mom called while I was still in my
pajamas. She wants me to visit next month.

Spent the afternoon at the bookstore on Powell. Picked up a copy of
Anna Karenina I will probably never finish and a used travel guide to
Japan. Sat in the cafe for two hours just reading and people-watching.
An elderly couple sat across from me sharing a single pastry and
speaking what sounded like Portuguese. I want that kind of slow,
unhurried companionship someday.

Made pasta for dinner. Nothing fancy — garlic, olive oil, red pepper
flakes, the last of the parmesan. Watched half of a documentary about
deep-sea creatures before falling asleep on the couch. Not a productive
day, but a good one.
