# Q4 Product Strategy Notes

## Where We Are

The product has strong retention among power users but acquisition has
plateaued. Our CAC is ~$45 and LTV is ~$180 for the core segment. The
onboarding flow is the biggest drop-off point — 62% of signups never
complete the first project setup.

## What We Should Do

The data points to three high-leverage moves this quarter:

1. **Onboarding overhaul.** Simplify the first-run experience from 7 steps
to 3. Add contextual tooltips and a progress indicator. Target: reduce
drop-off from 62% to 40%.

2. **Freemium pricing test.** Launch a limited free tier with 3 projects
and 1GB storage. Gate collaboration and analytics behind the Pro plan.
Monitor conversion rate over 60 days.

3. **API-first strategy.** Expose the core workflow engine as a REST API
so larger accounts can integrate with their existing tools. This opens
a high-ACV enterprise segment we have not yet addressed.

## Metrics To Watch

Weekly active users, project completion rate, time-to-first-project,
Pro plan conversion rate, API token provisioning count.
